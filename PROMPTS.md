# Prompts

## 1. Hero
Build a hero for NOOLAM, a Kanchipuram silk saree brand with a divine heritage-showroom feel.
Use a full-bleed image of a grand saree showroom with mannequins and silk-stacked shelves.
Add a glass pill navbar with logo, section links, wishlist, bag and a "Visit the Store" button.
Headline "Woven for the Divine." in large bold white type with a short line about temple looms.
Two buttons: "Try the Look" (maroon) and "Browse the Rack" (white), each with an arrow icon.
End with a maroon ticker strip scrolling gold section names like TRY THE LOOK, PURE ZARI, BRIDAL.

## 2. Six Moods
Create a "The NOOLAM Archive" section on a pure white background.
Headline "Six Moods. Timeless Drapes" with the second half in a maroon-to-gold gradient.
Show six saree photos framed inside golden temple-arch (gopuram) shaped windows with a finial on top.
Label each mood below in spaced capitals: Wedding Edit, Kanchipuram Icons, Banarasi Heirlooms, and more.
Add a short right-aligned line: "Woven by hand, worn for a lifetime."
Arches gently lift and glow on hover.

## 3. Try the Look
Build "Try the Look." with eight full-length models in styled sarees on a white stage.
Make it a drag/scroll carousel where the centre model is sharp and side models blur and fade.
On the right add an "In this look" panel listing every piece: saree, blouse, choker, jhumkas, ottiyanam, bangles.
Each item shows a thumbnail, name, price, a heart and a plus button.
At the bottom show a bundle price with 10% off and an "Add full look to bag" button.
Add a counter like "7 of 8 · Ivory Elephant" with arrows and a "Scroll or drag" hint.

## 4. Pick a Silk
Create "Pick a Silk" with a fanned arc of tall saree cards, each in a bold solid colour.
Each card has a huge condensed title behind the model (Palace Check, Bengal Garden, Kanchi Rani).
Model stands in front of the title with a soft shadow; price at bottom left, heart at top right.
A round black plus button drops the saree into the bag.
Cards tilt toward the cursor, press down on click and sit on a gentle curve.
Add a hint line: "Tilt a card, press it, poke the silk."

## 5. The Rack
Create "Every silk, on the rail." like a real store clothes rail.
Draw a thin chrome rod across the section with every product on a wooden hanger.
Add filter chips with counts: All pieces, Silk Sarees, Half Sarees, Blouses, Checked Sarees, Zari & Brocade.
Hovering a hanger brings it forward and shows its name and price below the rail.
Filtering slides hangers on and off the rail smoothly.
Add small captions: "Explore the collection · 9 pieces" and "AI-generated studio imagery".

## 6. Rack Detail
When a hanger is clicked, lift it off the rail and bring it to the centre, larger.
Blur the rest of the rail behind it.
Show product name in spaced capitals, price, and a Front / Back toggle.
Add size chips 34–42, a "Select a size" button, a heart and a "See availability" link.
Left and right arrows move to the next hanger; "Close ×" at top right.
Works the same for sarees and blouses.

## 7. The Muse Film
Create "The Muse Film" with the headline "Feel the drape" in a maroon-to-gold gradient.
Centre a 16:9 brand film inside a thin gold frame with corner brackets.
Add a small "Sound off" pill on the video to toggle audio.
On both sides, run vertical columns of lifestyle saree photos scrolling in opposite directions.
Fade the columns at top and bottom into white.
Below the film add a "Purchase the Collection" maroon button.

## 8. The Muse Reels
Create "Silk, in motion." with five saree reels filmed in real places.
Arrange vertical reel cards on a 3D curved arc, the centre one largest and facing forward.
Each card shows reel number, duration, fabric type, reel title and location.
Centre reel has a play button and a gold progress bar.
Add previous, pause and next controls at the top right.
Tapping the centre reel opens it full screen with sound.

## 9. The Bridal Collection
Create "The Bridal Collection" with a small line "For the muhurtham and every vow after".
Show bridal sarees in tall arch-topped cards on a curved carousel.
Centre card is upright with a gold border; side cards tilt away and fade.
Add small discount tags on some cards.
Below the centre card show saree name, fabric and pallu note, and price in gold.
Add prev/next arrows, an "Add to cart" button and a heart.
